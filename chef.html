Chef Profile

Chef Shierlyn Gasita

“A good bowl of ramen shouldn’t be rushed — not the broth, not the noodles, not the guest.”

Shierlyn learned the craft the old way — standing beside a ramen master from Osaka who relocated to Cainta during the pandemic, quietly passing down decades of broth and noodle technique to anyone patient enough to show up every day. That apprenticeship became the foundation for Gasita's, opened in 2024 to bring that same slow, unhurried bowl to Manila — built entirely from scratch, every single day.
